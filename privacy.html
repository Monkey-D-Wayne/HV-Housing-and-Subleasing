Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Rhedr (rhedr.com) is run by Boaz Cohen ("Rhedr", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Rhedr collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email support@rhedr.com.

1. What we collect

Browsing the housing map. You can browse buildings and sublets without an account. We don't ask for your name or email to browse.

Signing in. To post a sublet or contact a poster, you sign in with your school email (for Penn State, an @psu.edu address). We email you a one-time code. We store your email address, when your account was created and when you last signed in.

Posting a sublet. When you post, we store what you enter: listing type, building, street address, unit number, bedrooms and bathrooms, price, your lease rent if you add it, dates, furnished and utilities details, roommate notes, title, description and the first name you choose to show. We also store the map location of the address and any photos you upload.

Proof of residence. If you upload a lease page or bill (to earn the "Lease checked" badge, or because posting requires one), we store that file privately. It isn't shown to other users. To check it, we send the file to Anthropic, an AI service, which reads the names, address, landlord, lease dates and rent from it so we can compare them with your listing. Rhedr's administrator may also open the file to check it by hand.

We delete the file once it has been checked, and we delete any file still unchecked after 30 days. After the check we keep what was read from it except the names: the address, landlord, lease dates and rent, and whether it was approved. We use the rent and dates, without your name, to learn what places near campus really cost, for example to spot listings with unrealistic prices.

Messages. When you message another student on Rhedr, we store the messages, who sent them and when, the first name you chose to show, and whether you both tapped "We agreed on Rhedr". Before a message is saved, the site automatically hides phone numbers, email addresses, links and payment or social media handles in it, until you both agree. The same check hides them in listing descriptions. Nobody at Rhedr reads your chats as a routine. Rhedr's administrator reads a chat only if one of the two students reports it, or if the law or someone's safety requires it.

Reports. If you report a listing or a chat, we store the reason you give and which account sent it.

Sublet alerts. If you save a search to get alerts, we store what you asked for (for example bedrooms, price, distance, dates and building) with your account, and which sublets we've already emailed you, so you don't get the same one twice. We may also look at saved searches in total, without names or emails, to understand what students are looking for.

Emails to us. If you write to us, we keep the message and your email address so we can reply.

Technical information. Like most websites, the services that host and run Rhedr receive standard technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time. These logs are used to run the site, keep it secure and fix problems.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Rhedr currently doesn't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies; if that changes, we'll update this policy first.

2. How we use it

  • To run Rhedr: show listings, sign you in, save and display your sublets, and let signed-in students reach each other.
  • To keep the site safe: confirm that posters and repliers are students, check listings for scam patterns, review reports, verify lease proofs and remove listings or accounts that break the Terms of Use.
  • To contact you about your account or listings, for example sign-in codes or a question about a report.
  • To email you new sublets that match a search you saved, if you turned on alerts.
  • To improve Rhedr and fix problems.
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests.

3. Who can see your information

Anyone visiting the site can see a live listing's public details: title, description, building, street address, map location, price, dates, bedrooms and bathrooms, roommate notes, photos, the first name you chose to show and which checks it passed. Your email and unit number are not public.

The student you're chatting with sees your messages and the first name you chose. They see your email address (and, if you posted the sublet, your unit number) only after you both tap "We agreed on Rhedr" in that chat. Only share what you are comfortable sharing with other students.

Rhedr's administrator can see all account and listing data, including private lease proofs and reports.

Service providers that host and run Rhedr process data for us, listed below.

Legal reasons. We may share information if the law requires it, or if we believe it's needed to protect someone's safety, stop fraud, or protect Rhedr's rights.

If Rhedr changes hands. If Rhedr is reorganized, merged or sold, your information may move to the new owner, who would have to follow this policy or tell you about changes.

4. Services we rely on

Rhedr uses these outside services. When your browser loads them, they receive your IP address and standard browser data, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores accounts, listings, photos and lease proofs, and handles sign-in.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and other emails from the site, including alert emails and "you have a new message" emails, which show the start of the latest message.
  • Anthropic reads lease proofs for us with its AI (Claude) so we can check them. We send it the file only for that check.
  • Google Workspace handles email to and from rhedr.com.
  • Google Maps shows the map. If Google Maps isn't available, the map uses OpenStreetMap instead.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim turns the street address you type in a sublet post into a map location. The address you type is sent to it.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg) supply the site's fonts and scripts.
  • Building websites. Some building photos load directly from the building's or manager's own website, which receives a standard request from your browser when the photo appears.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Rhedr doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps a few things on your device so the site works: your sign-in session (so you stay signed in), and small preferences such as the housing type tab you last picked. Google Maps may set its own cookies or storage when the map loads. You can clear this anytime in your browser settings, which signs you out.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account while it's open. Listings you post are stored until you delete them, though listings past their end date stop showing publicly. Lease proof files are deleted once checked, or after 30 days if unchecked, as described above. Messages are kept while either student's account is open, because they're the record of what was agreed; they stay even if the listing is deleted. You can ask us to delete one sooner at any time. When you delete your account, we delete its data within a reasonable time, except reports and records of removed listings that we may keep to prevent fraud and repeat abuse, or data we must keep by law. Backups and hosting and service logs are kept for the periods those services set.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your listings anytime from "My sublets".
  • Turn off sublet alerts with the link at the bottom of any alert email, or under "My alerts".
  • Delete your account by emailing support@rhedr.com from your school email. We'll delete your account, listings, photos and lease proofs, except what we must keep for legal or safety reasons.
  • Ask what we have about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing the same address.

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states with privacy laws, or the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to some uses of it. To make a request, email us from the address on your account so we can confirm it's you. We'll respond as the law where you live requires, and we won't treat you differently for making a request.

8. Children

Rhedr is for college students. You must be at least 13 to use it, and users under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission to post or contact other users. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures, such as database access rules and encrypted connections, designed to keep private details, like your email, unit number and lease proof, limited to the people described above. No website or online service is completely secure, and we can't guarantee the security of your information. Please don't put sensitive information, such as bank details or ID numbers, in listings or messages.

10. Where data is stored

Rhedr is run from the United States, and our service providers store and process data in the United States. If you use Rhedr from another country, your information will be transferred to and handled in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Rhedr grows, for example when we add campuses or paid features. We'll change the date at the top, and for important changes we'll also give notice on the site or by email.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: support@rhedr.com.
